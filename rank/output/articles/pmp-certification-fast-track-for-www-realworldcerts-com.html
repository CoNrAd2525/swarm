PMP: Fast Track

Processes, artifacts, and exam strategy focused on scenario questions.

Fast Track Workflow

  1. Read the ECO and map domains to your weak areas
  2. Study agile + hybrid patterns first
  3. Do 20–30 scenario questions/day
  4. Review explanations; build a mistake log
  5. Full mock weekly; fix weakest domain

What to Practice Daily

  • “What should the PM do next?” scenarios
  • Stakeholder conflicts and communication choices
  • Risk/issue/change log decisions
  • Agile ceremonies and delivery tradeoffs

FAQs

What matters most on exam day? Scenario reasoning and choosing the next best action.

How do I improve quickly? Track mistakes by domain and revisit the same objective within 24–48 hours.
